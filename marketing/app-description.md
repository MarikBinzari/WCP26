# Predicto WCP26 — App Description (Marketing)

## 🇬🇧 English

**Predicto – The Ultimate World Cup 2026 Prediction Game**

Think you know football? Prove it.

Predicto is the go-to prediction app for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Predict match winners across the entire tournament — from the Group Stage through to the Final — and compete against friends, colleagues, and fans from around the world.

**What you can do:**
- 🎯 Predict every match result across two tournament phases: Groups and Knockout
- ⚽ Unlock weekly Exact Score challenges (every Sunday) for bonus points
- 🏆 Pick your tournament Champion and Top Scorer before the action begins
- 🥇 Create or join private Leagues with custom prizes and a password-protected entry
- 📊 Track your ranking on the live Leaderboard in real time
- 🔔 Get instant push notifications for match alerts and score updates
- 🌍 Available in English, Romanian, and French

Simple to play, impossible to stop. Download Predicto and show everyone who really knows the beautiful game.
